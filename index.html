Official MBSTU Lab Report & Assignment Cover Page Generator

The MBSTU Cover Page Generator is a free, web-based tool designed specifically for students at Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University (MBSTU) in Santosh, Tangail. Creating a standardized, professional academic cover page is a mandatory requirement for engineering laboratory reports and course assignments across departments such as Textile Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Information and Communication Technology, and others.

This generator streamlines the process into 4 simple steps: enter course details, input student and instructor info, manage lab experiment topics or assignment titles, and instantly preview and export a print-ready A4 vector PDF. Featuring the official MBSTU logo, automatic sequential batch numbering, and strict DD/MM/YYYY date formatting, students can generate multiple submission pages in under 2 minutes. The application runs entirely client-side in your browser, meaning it functions offline and ensures your personal information remains 100% private.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions about generating cover pages for MBSTU.

How do I get an MBSTU lab report cover page?

You can create and download an official MBSTU lab report cover page directly on this site in under 2 minutes. Simply complete the 4 steps: fill in your course information, your student and instructor details, your experiment numbers and topics, and click Download This PDF or Download All (ZIP).

Is the official MBSTU logo included?

Yes. The official Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University monogram is built directly into the generator in vector quality. You can also upload your own departmental logo or remove it if not needed.

Can I make assignment cover pages as well as lab reports?

Yes. In Step 3, use the document type switcher to toggle between Lab Report and Assignment mode. The titles, numbering, and topics adjust automatically to standard assignment formatting.

Is the generator free to use?

Yes, this tool is 100% free and open-source for all MBSTU students. There are no fees, subscriptions, or hidden charges.

Do I need to create an account or install anything?

No. No login or installation is needed. The tool runs directly in your browser on desktop or mobile and saves your inputs automatically in your local browser cache.
